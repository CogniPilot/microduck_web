Train Microduck in the browser

Augmented Random Search learns a linear correction (14 × 61 weights) added to the pretrained walking policy. Every episode is a real run of the whole-system FMU, from reset, for 3 s at 0.3 m/s forward command. The return is the trunk's forward progress, minus a small cost on the correction and a penalty for falling. Training happens in separate workers; the walking demo is unaffected.
